# EGO Gift Uptie Cost

Uptying an EGO gift in the Mirror Dungeon costs Cost (the in-run currency), paid at a shop's "Enhance Gifts" option. Cost depends on gift tier and how many uptie levels you apply at once. A multi-level uptie is just the sum of the individual steps.

| Tier | 0→1 | 1→2 | 0→2 |
|------|-----|-----|-----|
| 1    | 50  | 100 | 150 |
| 2    | 60  | 120 | 180 |
| 3    | 75  | 150 | 225 |
| 4    | 100 | 200 | 300 |

Not all EGO gifts can be enhanced. Tier 5/6 gifts (e.g. Lunar Vestige, Wealth) are rare specials and do not uptie.
